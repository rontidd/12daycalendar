# The label sheets

Print the PDFs at 100% with scaling off ("Actual size", never "Fit to page"),
then measure the 100 mm scale bar on the sheet before you cut anything.

- `inserts_face.pdf` — the face: the dates round the rim, the gifts inside,
  the weekday dial, the knob label and the two wheel tabs
- `inserts_face-option-a.pdf`, `inserts_face-option-b.pdf` — alternate faces
- `inserts_base.pdf` — the fixed feasts, one disc for the base

## Changing them

Every word on the sheets is live text, so you can retype the dates, the feast
names, the language, or your own family's twelve days. Free, open-source tools
that do it:

- **Inkscape** (inkscape.org) — the one to use. Open the PDF (in the import
  dialog, keep text as editable text), edit, and save as PDF again.
- **LibreOffice Draw** (libreoffice.org) — opens a PDF for small text changes.
- **GIMP** (gimp.org) — for painting over the artwork; open the PDF at 600 dpi
  and print the result at that resolution.

Whatever you use, keep the page at US Letter and don't move or resize the
pieces: every outline is the true size of the recess it drops into.
